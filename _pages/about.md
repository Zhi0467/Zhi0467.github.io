I am a PhD student at UC San Diego advised by [Tianhao Wang](https://tiiao.github.io). 

Three questions I'm working on as a PhD student:


1. **How do we monitor and steer agents to make agentic systems more reliable?** This connects to reliability, monitoring, and credit assignment. For example, what's the infrastructure and system needed for scientists to collaborate with agents? I'm building [RCP](https://github.com/Zhi0467/RCP), a control hub to make agents more reliable for scientific research, with humans in the loop and an auditable record of their work.
2. **How do we know if the optimization works before and during the compute going brrr?** I approach this through theory and predictive science: proving how optimization behaves, or forecasting what training will do to help set up and monitor it. Examples include adaptive optimizers and predicting or monitoring learning metrics.
3. **How to make agents perform robotic tasks reliably and with low latency?** I'm interested in tasks with different horizons and different demands on the speed of perception and action, how should the system orchestrate thinking and control?
